The important bits, minus the sales pitch.

01 Can I buy it yet? +

Not yet. Well Mudda S____ is in product development. We are evaluating supplier samples, packaging, manufacturing economics and demand before accepting orders.

02 Is it made in The Bahamas? +

It is a Bahamian-founded brand. We are exploring third-party overseas manufacturing and packaging, with the finished goods imported into The Bahamas. Final country of manufacture will be disclosed once selected.

03 What will the first product be? +

The proposed first release is a 3-ply tissue in a 12-roll consumer pack. All specifications and formats are provisional until manufacturing and product validation are complete.

04 Will hotels and villas be able to order? +

We want to speak with villa managers, short-term rental operators and hospitality buyers about purchasing needs, formats and distribution. Commercial sales have not started.

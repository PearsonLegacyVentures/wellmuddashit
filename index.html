01 Can I buy Well Mudda S____ yet? +

Not yet. We're still developing the product, evaluating manufacturing options and validating the numbers before accepting orders.

02 Is it made in The Bahamas? +

Well Mudda S____ is a Bahamian-born brand. Overseas private-label manufacturing is being explored. We'll be clear about the final country of manufacture when it's confirmed.

03 What will the first product be? +

We're targeting a premium-positioned 3-ply tissue in a 12-roll consumer pack. Sheet count, materials, texture and final packaging are still under evaluation.

04 Can hotels and villas register interest? +

Yes. We're keen to understand ordering quantities, storage needs, purchasing cycles and potential demand from hospitality operators. Enquiries are not purchase commitments.
